## Design system

direction: new

This codifies the placeholder look that slices A and B shipped (system-ui, white page, one link blue) as a written system, and adds only what the category page needs. Nothing existing changes value. Approving it records it in `docs/design/system.md`.

### Principles

1. **Scan, then click.** The visitor knows what they want. Every list reads in one fixed order, in the same place on every row: what it is, then what it costs, then where it goes.
2. **The price is the truth.** Prices are final, IVA included, and the page says so once, near the top. No shipping, no totals.
3. **Plain beats clever.** One typeface, one link colour, one border colour, no shadows, no motion. Trust comes from consistency.
4. **The one bold element is the brand, not the list.** It is reserved for the header and home (S5.2). Nothing on a list or an offer row competes for attention.

## Flow

Who: a consumer or taller who already knows the kind of part or tool and is comparing. The job here: find the product in the category and open it. What they need first: the category name, then a scannable list where each product shows what it is and its lowest price.

1. Arrive on `/categoria/<slug>` (from a search engine, a link, or later the home page in S5.2). First read: the h1, then the two muted lines (count and "Precios con IVA incluido.").
2. Scan the cards: title first, then brand, then the price and shop count at the bottom.
3. Open a product: click or tap anywhere on a card. Leaves to `/producto/<slug>`, where the shop links live. The card never links to a shop.
4. Or page through: "Siguiente" and "Anterior" at the bottom, which reload the page with `?pagina=<n>`. Page 1 is the clean URL.
5. Dead ends and where each leaves: empty category goes to home through "Volver al inicio"; unknown slug or a page past the last gives the existing 404 screen, which goes home through "Volver al inicio"; the header site name goes home from everywhere.

First-time visitor: nothing to learn or dismiss; no onboarding, no banner. The IVA line is how a new visitor learns that prices are final.

## Accessibility floor

- **Focus visible.** Every interactive element shows `--focus-ring` on `:focus-visible`: the card (ring around the whole card, 2 px offset), the pagination links, the header name and the "Volver al inicio" link. No `outline: none` without a replacement.
- **Focus order.** Header link, then the cards in document order (row by row, left to right, same as the visual order), then "Anterior", then "Siguiente". One tab stop per card; no `tabindex` above 0.
- **Semantics.** One h1 (the category name). Cards are `<li>` in a `<ul>`; each card title is an `<h2>`. Pagination is a `<nav aria-label="Paginación">`; the indicator is plain text and is not a link. The empty state heading is an `<h2>` under the h1.
- **Contrast.** Text at least 4.5:1 (muted 7.5:1). The pagination link border and the ring are `--color-link` (6.9:1). The grey card outline is decorative; the card is identified by its text and the ring, not by the outline.
- **Targets.** Cards are the full card area (well over 44 px). Pagination links 44 px high and at least 44 px wide.
- **Text alternatives.** No images on the page; the arrows are `aria-hidden`.
- **Motion.** None, so nothing to reduce.
- **Zoom.** Text sizes are in rem, and the grid reflows to one column at narrow widths; the page does not scroll sideways at 320 px.

## Checks

For UX-REVIEW on the rendered page, at 390 and 1280. "Phone" is a 390 px screenshot, "desktop" is a 1280 px one. A check marked (DOM) or (CSS) is a consistency check, not a screenshot one.

1. **Heading.** The first and largest text under the header is the category name. False if the heading is missing, shows the slug, or the site name is bigger.
2. **Summary and IVA line.** Under the h1 are two muted lines: the product count (plural right: "1 producto", "2 productos") and "Precios con IVA incluido.", in that order. False if either line is missing, reordered, or the IVA line appears inside the cards.
3. **Phone layout.** One column of full-width cards in a 342 px content area; the full-page screenshot is exactly 390 px wide with no horizontal scroll. False if cards are two-up, clipped or overflowing.
4. **Desktop layout.** Three equal columns whose left edge lines up with the h1 at the same left edge as the product page content; rows have equal-height cards. False if a different count of columns appears at 1280, or cards in a row differ in height.
5. **Card order.** Each card reads title, then brand (if any), then a footer with price and shop count at the card bottom. False if the price is above the title, or footers in one row sit at different heights.
6. **Price match.** A card's price equals the first (cheapest) price on that product's page, formatted `11,49 €` with a euro sign after the number. False if the two differ or the format is `€11.49`, `11.49` or `11,49€` without space.
7. **No shipping, no total.** No word about envío, no sum, no other price on a card. False if any appears.
8. **One shop.** A single-offer product reads "1 tienda" with no "Desde". False if it reads "0 tiendas", "1 tiendas" or shows "Desde".
9. **Several shops.** A multi-offer product reads "Desde <price>" and "N tiendas". False if "Desde" is missing or the count is wrong.
10. **No brand.** A card without brand has the title then the footer directly: no blank line, "null", "undefined", dash or empty gap. False if any is visible.
11. **Long title.** A title longer than three lines is clamped at three with an ellipsis and the card does not grow beyond that. False if the card is taller than its three-line neighbours would allow or text is cut mid-box without an ellipsis.
12. **Unbroken string.** A title with a very long word stays inside the card. False if the card or the page scrolls sideways.
13. **Whole card is one link.** (DOM) Each card holds exactly one `<a>`, whose `href` starts with `/producto/`, and no `/go/` link. False if a card has two links or a `/go` link.
14. **Pagination layout.** On a middle page the phone screenshot shows "Anterior" at the left, "Página X de Y" centred and "Siguiente" at the right, each link at least 44 px high. False if the order differs, a link is shorter than 44 px, or the indicator is a link.
15. **Pagination edges.** Page 1 shows no "Anterior" and the indicator is still centred; the last page shows no "Siguiente". False if a disabled or dead link is rendered, or the indicator shifts to the edge.
16. **Single page.** A category with one page shows no pagination row and no "Página 1 de 1". False if either appears.
17. **Paged summary.** On page 2 or later the summary line reads "N productos · Página X de Y". False if the page part is missing on a paged category, or present on a single-page one.
18. **Clean first page.** (DOM) The "Anterior" link on page 2 points to the category URL with no query. False if it ends in `?pagina=1`.
19. **Empty state.** An empty category shows the h1, the heading "Aún no hay productos en esta categoría", the sentence, and "Volver al inicio", with no count, no grid and no pagination, status 200. False if any of those is absent or extra.
20. **404.** An unknown slug and a page past the last both show the existing "Página no encontrada" screen with status 404. False if either shows the empty state, a blank page or an error.
21. **Invalid page.** `?pagina=0` and `?pagina=abc` render the same content as no query. False if the cards differ, or the page errors.
22. **No images.** No grey placeholder square or empty image box on any card. False if one appears.
23. **Same product.** The category page uses the same typeface, text colour, link blue and left content edge as the product page (compare with `00-product-desktop.png`). False if any differs.
24. **Hover.** (CSS) On hover the card border turns `--color-link` and the title underlines; the card has no shadow or movement. False if the rules are missing or add a shadow or transform.
25. **Focus ring.** (CSS, consistency check) The card and the pagination links define a 3 px `--color-link` ring on `:focus-visible` with no bare `outline: none`. False if absent or removed.
26. **Focus order.** (DOM, consistency check) Document order of focusable elements is: header link, one link per card in visual order, "Anterior", "Siguiente"; no positive `tabindex`. False if a card has two tab stops or the order differs from the reading order.
27. **Copy.** Every string in § Copy appears exactly as written, with the correct accents and plurals, and no string outside `messages/es-ES.json`. False if any wording differs or English or a raw message key shows.
28. **Server-rendered.** (DOM) The HTML response of a populated category contains the product titles with no script run. False if the list is absent from the response.
