### Principles

1. **Scan, then click.** The visitor knows what they want. Every list reads in one fixed order, in the same place on every row: what it is, then what it costs (or how much is there), then where it goes.
2. **The price is the truth.** Prices are final, IVA included, and a page that shows prices says so once, near the top. No shipping, no totals.
3. **Plain beats clever.** One typeface, one link colour, one border colour, no shadows, no motion. Trust comes from consistency.
4. **The one bold element is the brand, not the list.** NEW in use: it is now spent, once, on the brand panel on the home page. Nothing on a list, a category group or an offer row competes for attention, and no other page gets a filled panel.

## Which categories appear

This is a UX rule, settled here. The tech lead implements it and records the one invariant in the contract.

**Rule.** A category appears on the home page if and only if its own category page would show a product count of 1 or more, that is, public products in the category and every descendant, by the one visibility scope. The home never links to a page that would say "Aún no hay productos en esta categoría".

Cases:

| Case | What the home shows |
|---|---|
| Top-level category with products in its children, none of its own | shown; lists only the children that have products |
| Top-level category with products of its own and no children with products | shown as a heading row only, no rows under it |
| Top-level category with no products anywhere in its subtree | not shown |
| Child with no products under a shown parent | not shown (no greyed-out row, no "0 productos") |
| Every category hidden by this rule | the no-products state (§ Screens D) |
| Admin hides a product, or disables a feed, and a category becomes empty | it leaves the home on the next request; nothing is cached across requests |

Why. The visitor came to reach products; a link to an empty page costs a click and some trust, and the catalogue starts thin, so a visible tree of 30 categories with 20 empty is worse than a short one. Guardrail 1 is served for free: a hidden or unapproved item can never keep a category visible.

The demo-categories open point from the backlog revision is covered. Once demo feeds are disabled at S9.4 their products stop being public, every demo category counts zero, and the home shows the no-products state (or the real categories alone). The empty demo category `aceites-y-lubricantes` is already hidden today for the same reason; its page keeps answering 200 with its empty state when opened by URL.

Depth. The home shows two levels: top-level categories (no parent) and their direct children. Deeper levels are not listed; their products still count in their ancestors' counts.

Order. By the admin's `position`, then by name; the same at both levels.

## Flow

Who: a consumer or taller who knows the kind of part or tool they need and arrives without a product in mind, or from the site name link. The job here: reach the right category in one tap. What they need to see first: that this is a price comparison for Spain (the panel), then the list of categories.

1. Arrive on `/`. First read: the brand panel (site name, one line saying what the site does). Second: the "Categorías" heading. Third: the groups.
2. Scan the groups: each top-level category is a heading with its product count; its children are rows under it, each with a count.
3. Tap a child row, or a group heading. Leaves to `/categoria/<slug>`, which lists products (S5.1). The heading link opens the parent category, which includes its children's products.
4. From any page, the site name in the header leaves for `/`.
5. Dead ends. No products at all: the no-products state has no link; the visitor can only wait or leave. Everything else leaves through a category link.

First-time visitor: identical to a returning one; the panel is the only explanation and it is one line. No onboarding, no banner, no search yet (S5.4).

## Accessibility floor

- **Focus visible.** Every link shows `--focus-ring` on `:focus-visible`: the header name (2 px offset), group headings and child rows (inset, offset -3 px). No `outline: none` without a replacement.
- **Focus order.** Header link, then each group in document order: heading link, then its child links, then the next group. On desktop the groups read left to right, row by row, the same as the DOM. No `tabindex` above 0; no skip link (there is only one link before the content).
- **Semantics.** One h1 (the site name, in the panel). The "Categorías" h2, then one h3 per group, each containing the one heading link. Groups are `<li>` of a `<ul>`; each group's children are a nested `<ul>`. The no-products state replaces the h2 with its own h2. The count is part of the link text.
- **Contrast.** White on `--color-link` is 6.9:1 (panel text); link text on white 6.9:1; muted counts 7.5:1; body 17.4:1. The group outline and dividers are decorative: a row is identified by its text and its blue colour, never by the grey line.
- **Targets.** Every row, every heading row and the header link are at least 44 px tall and the full width of their box.
- **Text alternatives.** No images and no decorative glyphs on the home page.
- **Motion.** None.
- **Zoom.** Sizes in rem; the grid reflows to one column; no sideways scroll at 320 px.
