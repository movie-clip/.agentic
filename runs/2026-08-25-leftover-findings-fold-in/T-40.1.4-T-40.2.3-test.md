(App.test.tsx). Inserted one mocked fetch response for `POST
/api/portfolios/import/combine-snapshots` between the existing 5th
(`ffBootstrapPayload`) and 6th (`diagnosticsPayload`) `mockResolvedValueOnce`

`formatVariantNodeLabel` change (AC1/AC2). `variantSnapshot` spreads the same
`importedAt` unchanged, so the variant node carries the identical date.
